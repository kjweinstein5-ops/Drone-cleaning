⚠ Verify before you spend — none of this can be settled from a desk

  1. Will Inspired Flight support a liquid spray payload? Warranty and airworthiness. This can disqualify the aircraft outright — ask before anything else.
  2. Skydio radiometric export. Can you get raw per-pixel temperature out? A colourised JPEG is useless to the fusion pipeline.
  3. Blue UAS status on the DCMA list itself, not vendor pages — and which FCC exemption each purchase relies on. Both exemptions expire 1 Jan 2027.
  4. Insurance for a self-integrated spray drone vs a vendor-supported one. Still unasked, still likely the deciding number.
  5. IF1200A lead time — currently listed sold out.
  6. X10 vs X10D — commercial vs defense SKU; price and capability differ.
