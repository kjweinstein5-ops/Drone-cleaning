Step 03 · Plan

Recaulk the joint

Time ~45 min Difficulty 2/5 Est. cost $31
  1. Cut & pull out the old caulk with a removal tool.
  2. Clean & dry the joint; wipe with rubbing alcohol.
  3. Tape both edges for a clean line.
  4. Apply a continuous bead of mildew-resistant silicone.
  5. Tool the bead, pull tape, cure 24 h before water exposure.
