02 / 12
The problem

The person with the problem isn't the person with the expertise

  • They don't know what's actually wrong.
  • They don't know what the fix involves — steps, tools, skill, risk.
  • They don't know what to buy out of 40 near-identical SKUs.
  • They don't know whether to DIY or hire, or what's fair to pay.

Result: paralysis, abandoned projects, wrong purchases, returns — and intent is highest at the exact moment no tool helps.

The product

Five modes, one loop

Point → Diagnose → Plan → Choose → Execute / Delegate → Verify.

01 · Diagnose

AI names the object, the problem, and the severity.

02 · Measure

LiDAR captures the dimensions of the work zone.

03 · Plan

Steps, tools, materials, cost, time, difficulty.

04 · Choose

The exact SKUs to buy, previewed in AR.

05 · Execute

AR step guidance, or one-tap handoff to a vetted pro.

Business model

Five ways to earn at peak intent

  • Freemium → Pro — unlimited plans + AR execution guidance (~$9–14/mo).
  • Brand placement — labeled sponsored SKU slot (highest margin).
  • Affiliate — buy-buttons to Home Depot / Lowe's / Amazon.
  • SDK / white-label — retailers embed our diagnosis engine.
  • Contractor Pro — scoped, high-intent leads + project management.

Trust guardrail: sponsored is always labeled; a neutral Best Fit always shown alongside.
